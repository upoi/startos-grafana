# Grafana

Grafana allows you to query, visualize, alert on and understand your metrics no matter where they are stored. Create, explore, and share beautiful dashboards with your team and foster a data-driven culture.

## Preconfigured Data Sources
This package automatically connects to the **Prometheus** service within your StartOS instance natively.

## Default Dashboards
No dashboards are pre-installed in this deployment. You can import your own or connect Grafana to other services manually.
